Local Resources for Cat Care

  • PAWS – Low-Cost Vet Clinics
    Provides affordable veterinary care and supports pet owners in underserved communities across Philadelphia.
  • The Cat Doctor – Low-Cost Vet Clinics
    A feline-exclusive veterinary hospital offering high-quality, compassionate care.
  • Philly Pet Food Pantry – Food Bank
    Supplies free pet food to families in need so they can continue to care for their pets.
  • Forgotten Cats – TNR Services
    Specializes in Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) and adoption services to control the feral cat population.
  • Project MEOW – TNR Services
    A volunteer-based organization helping West Philadelphia with TNR, fostering, and community cat support.
