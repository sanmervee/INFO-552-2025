Tiger Finds a Family

Tiger was a shy orange tabby living under a porch in Powelton Village. Thanks to the registry, Sarah and Racheal connected and worked together to feed and shelter him. Within a few weeks, Milo warmed up and found a permanent home with Racheal.

Rosie’s Winter Shelter

Rosie was spotted by multiple neighbors during the harsh winter months. Using the website, they coordinated a warm shelter and feeding schedule. Rosie stayed healthy through the season and was later spayed and released by a local rescue.
