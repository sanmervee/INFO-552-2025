About Philly Stray Cats Hub

Philly Stray Cats Hub is a community-driven website built to help Philadelphia residents care for outdoor and stray cats in their neighborhoods.

We know how confusing it can be to see a cat outside and wonder: “Is this cat a stray? Is someone already feeding it?” Or maybe you’re feeding a neighborhood cat but can’t always manage it alone. This site was created to help answer those questions—and connect neighbors who care.

Our main goals are:

  • To help identify and register outdoor cats so neighbors can share information
  • To make it easy to request or offer help with feeding, sheltering, or vet visits
  • To build a local support network of cat lovers and caretakers in Philadelphia

This is not a rescue group or shelter. It's a simple, user-friendly tool made for everyday people who want to help, even if it's just one cat at a time.

We believe small acts of kindness add up—and together, we can make a big difference for the stray cats in our city.
