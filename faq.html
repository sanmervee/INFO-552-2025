Frequently Asked Questions

What is this website for?

This site helps Philadelphia residents identify outdoor cats and coordinate care with neighbors. It acts as a central place to register cats, ask for help, and connect with others who care.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. All forms are open to the public to keep things simple and accessible.

How do I know if a cat is already being cared for?

Use the Identify/Register a Cat page. You can search for cats others have registered or add your own to help others identify them.

What if I need help feeding or sheltering a cat?

You can post a help request using the Request/Offer Help page. Others in your neighborhood can see your request and offer to co-care.

Is this a rescue organization?

No, this site is a local community tool—not a rescue group. It’s designed to support everyday neighbors who care about stray cats and want to take action together.

Can I add a photo of a cat?

Yes! When registering a cat or requesting help, you’ll be able to upload a photo to help others recognize the cat.

Where can I find affordable vet services or food?

Check the Local Resources page. We’ve listed trusted local clinics, food pantries, and TNR services available in Philadelphia.
